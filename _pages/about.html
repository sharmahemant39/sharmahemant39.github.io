Assistant Professor & Head of MathematicsTungal School of Basic and Applied Sciences

I work at the intersection of statistics, image processing and numerical linear algebra. My research interests include tensor covariance estimation, Stein shrinkage, tensor computations and eigenvalue analysis.

I am completing my Ph.D. in Mathematics at IIITDM Kancheepuram; my thesis has been submitted. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from VNIT Nagpur and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi.

I welcome teaching and faculty opportunities, postdoctoral positions and research collaborations in these areas.

Publications

Research interests: statistics, image processing and numerical linear algebra, with a focus on tensor covariance estimation, shrinkage methods and spectral analysis. My work on Stein shrinkage connects statistical estimation with the T-product framework for tensors.

Teaching & Experience

As a third-generation mathematics teacher in my family, I value clear explanations, geometric intuition and mathematical rigour. I use visual reasoning, worked examples and connections between concepts to help students understand abstract mathematics.

My teaching experience spans mathematics and engineering programmes, including Linear Algebra, Numerical Methods, Calculus and Geometry. I aim to connect theory with computation while helping students develop logical reasoning and problem-solving skills.
